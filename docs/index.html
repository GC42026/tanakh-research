Tanakh Research

An open research journal on Tanakh, Zohar, and Kabbalah.

What does it mean for the whole to be present in the individual? When Tanakh tells a story about a family, a people, or a world, who is undergoing that story within the person?

We begin with the Hebrew and its context, then follow how Jewish commentators, midrash, and the Zohar open further readings. Each entry gives the sources and makes clear where a reading becomes an open question.
